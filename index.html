Frequently Asked Questions

Which platforms does PostAway support?+
PostAway supports Instagram (Business or Creator accounts) and TikTok directly via official APIs.
Can I cancel anytime?+
Yes, you can easily manage or cancel your subscription at any time directly from your Profile settings.
Does the free trial require a credit card?+
The Free account requires no card. Paid trial plans require a payment method, but you won't be charged until Day 6.
How does auto-publishing work?+
You connect your social accounts via secure OAuth. Once you schedule a post, PostAway automatically publishes it at the exact chosen time.
What Instagram account type is required?+
An Instagram Business or Creator account connected to a Facebook Page is required for direct auto-publishing. Switching your account type is free inside Instagram.
