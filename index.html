Frequently asked questions

Which platforms does PostAway support? +
PostAway currently supports Instagram (Business and Creator accounts) and TikTok. Both platforms support auto-publishing, scheduling, and analytics.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime? +
Yes, cancel anytime from your Profile settings. No long-term commitments required.
Does the free trial require a credit card? +
Creating a free account requires no credit card. If you upgrade to a paid plan, the 5-day trial is included and you won't be charged until it ends.
How does auto-publishing work? +
Connect your Instagram and TikTok accounts via OAuth, schedule your posts with captions and media, and PostAway publishes them at the exact time you set - no manual action needed. You'll get an email confirmation when each post goes live.
What kind of accounts can connect to Instagram? +
Instagram requires a Business or Creator account connected to a Facebook Page to use the publishing API. Personal accounts cannot be connected. Switching is free and takes about 2 minutes in the Instagram app.

How to schedule a post

1
Tap Schedule in the bottom nav
Go to the Schedule tab and tap the + button to start a new post.
2
Upload your photos or video
Select media from your camera roll. You can add multiple images for a carousel.
3
Let AI write your caption
Tap "Generate Caption" and PostAway will write a caption and hashtags based on your content.
4
Pick a date and time, then schedule
Choose when to post and hit Schedule. PostAway handles the rest automatically.
🔀

How to use the Randomizer

1
Go to the Randomizer tab
Tap the shuffle icon in the bottom nav to open the Randomizer.
2
Upload a batch of photos
Add all the photos you want to post - old content, new content, anything in your library.
3
Tap Randomize
PostAway shuffles your photos into a fresh posting order so your grid never looks repetitive.
4
Send to Schedule
Happy with the order? Send the whole batch to Schedule with one tap.
